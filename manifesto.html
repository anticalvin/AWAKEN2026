The Awakening Manifesto

Short version: pay attention to what has your attention.

I. The Feed

You didn't choose most of what you saw today. It was ranked, tested and served to you, because your attention is how someone else gets paid. That isn't a conspiracy. It's a business model, and it works.

II. The Fog

Scroll long enough and everything blurs: the same opinions, the same outrage, the same shirt. You can feel it. You just can't name it. We think that feeling is worth listening to.

III. Awake

Awake doesn't mean angry, and it doesn't mean certain. It means checking before you share, asking who profits, and being fine with not having a take. Question everything. Start with us.

IV. The Cult

Yes, we know what the name sounds like. That's the joke, and the joke has a serious side: if someone is going to sell you a belief system, you should at least know you're being sold one. Ours comes as clothing. Wear it, and let people ask.

"Pay attention to what has your attention."

The five rules

  1. Check before you share.
  2. Ask who profits.
  3. Make something.
  4. Be decent in person.
  5. Repeat.
